Caution signals:
1. The submitted URL is an affiliate-controlled GetResponse landing page, not the official site.
2. “Free” can omit the practical importance of paid upgrades and recurring offers.
3. Earnings are not guaranteed.
4. The complete upgrade ladder, autoresponder costs, refund rules, and lead-export/control conditions require verification before purchase or promotion.
5. The opportunity substantially overlaps with other funnel/list-building programs and may create unnecessary duplicated expenses.
