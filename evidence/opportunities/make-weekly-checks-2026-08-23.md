## Observed URLs

- `https://makeweeklychecks.com/get-weekly-paychecks?utm_source=private&utm_medium=solo&utm_campaign=elpbackup`
- Personalized thank-you route: `https://makeweeklychecks.com/get-weekly-paychecks-typ-[redacted]?awt_m=[redacted]&awt_email=[EMAIL_REDACTED]`

The personalized URL supplied by the user contained a full email address. It is intentionally redacted because this repository is public.

## Evidence observed

- The first URL identifies its source as a private solo-ad campaign.
- The personalized URL contains autoresponder-style parameters including `awt_m` and `awt_email`.
- The thank-you-page slug includes `typ`, consistent with a post-opt-in marketing step.
- Search results associate the domain with videos promoting escape from employment income and additional income streams.
- No clearly identified employer, compensation agreement, or independently verified earnings documentation was located.
- Automated third-party review previously assigned the domain a low trust score and raised phishing/spam-related concerns. Automated reputation scores are supporting signals, not proof by themselves.

## Risk indicators

1. Income-oriented “weekly paychecks” framing may be mistaken for employment or predictable compensation.
2. Solo-ad acquisition indicates an affiliate or business-opportunity funnel.
3. The personalized link exposes and tracks the recipient's email address.
4. The seller, full pricing ladder, required work, upsells, and refund conditions were not transparently verifiable.
5. The personalized route was rejected by a safe-browsing inspection tool as unsafe to open.
