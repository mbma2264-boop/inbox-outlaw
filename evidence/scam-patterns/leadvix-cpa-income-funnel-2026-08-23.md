4. **Tracking attribution:** The `tracking_slug` identifies the referring campaign or account.
5. **New domain:** The domain had only a short operating history when captured.
6. **Hidden ownership:** No transparent operator identity was verified.
7. **Privacy contradiction:** “We hate spam” and “privacy is respected” appear beside a form operated by a lead marketplace.

## Detection rules / signals to incorporate

Increase risk when combinations include:
- A large daily-income figure near an email-only access gate.
- “Free video” or “instant access” paired with tracking attribution.
- A platform selling verified leads while simultaneously collecting consumer opt-ins.
- Missing or hard-to-find company, privacy, earnings, and refund disclosures.
- A recently registered domain making substantial income claims.

## Recommended handling

Do not submit an email address, phone number, payment information, passwords, banking details, or identity documents. Do not purchase or promote the lead service without verified ownership, privacy terms, lead-consent records, refund terms, and evidence supporting its income representations.
